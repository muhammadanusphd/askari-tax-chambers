Our Areas of Practice

Askari Tax Chambers, led by Najam H. Askari, Advocate High Court, provides professional legal and tax services to individuals, businesses and organizations. With legal practice since 2019, the Chambers handles a broad range of civil, criminal, tax, commercial, corporate, property and regulatory matters with a focus on practical legal solutions and effective representation.

Tax Law

Legal and tax assistance relating to income tax, sales tax, tax notices, assessments, appeals, recovery matters and other taxation-related disputes.

Income Tax

Assistance concerning income tax matters, tax notices, assessments, declarations, appeals, compliance and representation before relevant authorities.

Sales Tax

Legal assistance regarding sales tax matters, notices, assessments, registration issues, compliance and disputes before the relevant tax forums.

Tax Appeals & Litigation

Representation and legal assistance in tax disputes, appeals, recovery proceedings and litigation before competent tax authorities and courts.

Civil Law

Representation in civil disputes involving contracts, recovery, declarations, injunctions, damages and other civil remedies.

Criminal Law

Legal representation in criminal matters, including bail, FIR-related proceedings, investigations, trials and criminal appeals.

Family Law

Assistance in family disputes involving marriage, dissolution of marriage, maintenance, guardianship, custody and related family matters.

Property Law

Legal assistance concerning ownership, possession, property disputes, sale and purchase, tenancy and property-related litigation.

Corporate Law

Legal assistance for businesses and organizations involving contracts, corporate documentation, governance and commercial legal matters.

Commercial Law

Assistance with commercial transactions, business disputes, contractual obligations, recovery matters and other commercial legal issues.

Banking & Financial Law

Legal assistance in banking disputes, financial transactions, recovery matters, documentation and related regulatory issues.

Labour & Employment Law

Assistance concerning employment disputes, service matters, workplace issues, contracts and other labour-related legal proceedings.

Legal Documentation

Preparation and review of legal agreements, contracts, notices, applications, affidavits and other legal documentation.

Constitutional Law

Legal research and representation concerning constitutional rights, public law matters and constitutional remedies before competent courts.

Service Matters

Assistance in employment and service-related disputes, departmental proceedings, disciplinary matters and related legal remedies.

Legal Consultancy

Professional legal consultation for individuals, businesses and organizations requiring practical advice on legal and regulatory matters.
