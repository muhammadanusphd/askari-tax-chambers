Welcome to Askari Tax Chambers

Professional Legal, Tax & Corporate Advisory Services

Askari Tax Chambers is a law firm based in Toba Tek Singh, providing professional legal, tax, corporate, commercial and advisory services to individuals, businesses and organizations.

The Chambers is led by Najam H. Askari, Advocate High Court, who has been practising law since 2019. The firm's approach combines legal knowledge, practical advocacy and strategic advice to help clients understand and address their legal and regulatory matters.

Our services cover a broad range of legal matters including taxation, corporate and commercial affairs, civil litigation, property matters, family law, criminal defence, banking and financial matters, employment issues and general legal consultancy.
